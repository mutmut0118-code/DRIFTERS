# Task 9: crossbows (any CrossbowItem) loaded, aimed and fired as a player does; and a launcher that never looses is given up on (ruling R23).
# python tools/smoke.py . tools/smoke-gear-crossbow.txt build/smoke-gear-crossbow.log
# Fresh world, flat (server.properties level-type=minecraft\:flat for this run), by day, no mob spawning, optional mods in run/mods (Simply Bows,
# Too Many Bows). The skeletons wear iron helmets (no burning by day; at night the Drifters go out hunting and walk right up to them) and are
# given their bows (summoned with tags, a skeleton comes empty-handed and runs in to punch). Each Drifter is 60 blocks from the others.
# Expect:
#  1  Cross (brain on, brave) with only a crossbow, 16 arrows and a stone sword, a skeleton 15 off: "loaded its Crossbow" before each shot,
#     "got it (shoot the skeleton, N shot(s))", "the fight is over"; arrows fewer than 16 ("drifter inventory": loading used them).
#     A second skeleton after the first dies: the same again (a crossbow left loaded fires with no "loaded" line before it). Hurt by then, it
#     may run from the second instead (its health decides).
#  2  Multi, the same with a Multishot crossbow: one arrow spent per load, three bolts per shot;
#     "drifter gear item minecraft:crossbow": "powers: none" (R21: the enchantment explains the volley, so nothing is learned about the item).
#  3  Ready, a crossbow given already loaded and 8 arrows: its first shot has no "loaded" line before it (a loaded crossbow fires at once).
#  4  R23, Simply Bows first: Ice (brain off) with simplybows:ice_bow/ice_bow and Earth with simplybows:earth_bow/earth_bow, "drifter shoot" at a
#     standing pillager 14 off: arrows go (fewer than 64 + 64), no "gave up on" line.
#  5  R23, Too Many Bows (disabled in the user's game; its classes name client-only ones, so a dedicated server can't look into them):
#     Flame with too_many_bows:flame_bow shoots (its helper player lets go: arrows fewer than 64 + 64, "found out Pyre's Embrace: fire");
#     Dud with too_many_bows:radiance (it spends a player's levels; Dud has none) looses nothing: it draws longer each time (20, 40 ... 100
#     ticks: ruling R29, a modded bow may need a longer draw), then 3 more full-length draws, then "gave up on Radiance: it won't shoot"
#     (about 30 s: the first shoot may time out and the second finish it), and the last "drifter shoot" fails at once ("nothing to shoot with").
